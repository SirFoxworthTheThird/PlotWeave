# Moby-Dick example sources

## Text and structure

- Herman Melville, *Moby-Dick; or, The Whale*, Project Gutenberg ebook 2701: <https://www.gutenberg.org/files/2701/2701-h/2701-h.htm>
- The example follows all 135 author-titled chapters and the Epilogue. Its summaries are original structural writing and do not reproduce the novel's prose.
- The calendar is an editorial chronology. It preserves narrative order and plausible elapsed time without claiming that Melville supplies exact dates for the voyage.

## Original generated artwork

All files under `art/` and `maps/` were generated specifically for this example on 4 September 2026. They were visually reviewed in contact sheets before release.

- `art/world.png`: library cover showing the Pequod and the white sperm whale.
- `art/characters/`: twenty-one distinct character portraits.
- `art/items/`: twelve distinct object studies.
- `art/locations/`: twenty-six distinct place illustrations.
- `art/factions/`: four distinct group illustrations.
- `maps/world-voyage.png`: period-style world voyage chart.
- `maps/new-england.png`: pre-canal southeastern Massachusetts and Nantucket chart. An initial version containing the anachronistic Cape Cod Canal was rejected and corrected.
- `maps/pequod-deck.png`: navigable weather-deck plan.
- `maps/pequod-below-decks.png`: navigable interior plan.

Maps are used only as navigational layers. Portraits, item images, location illustrations, faction covers, and the world cover use separate purpose-made artwork.
